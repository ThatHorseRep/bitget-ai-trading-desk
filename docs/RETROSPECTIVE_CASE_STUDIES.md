# Illustrative Scenario Walkthroughs: Pre-Trade Risk Stress Testing

> **What this document is — and is not.**
> These are **illustrative scenario walkthroughs**, not audited historical trade records. Each walkthrough runs a realistic trader thesis through the desk's deterministic stress engine under a stated set of market conditions, so a reader can see exactly how the engine behaves and why the desk policy produces its verdict.
>
> **Why not historical trades?** Bitget's tokenized-equity (Reality / rToken) platform launched in **May 2026**. Any claim of audited 2024 rTSLA/rNVDA/rCOIN P&L outcomes would be fabricated, and we will not present fabricated results. The dollar outcomes below are the **arithmetic output of the production engine** (`src/core/scenarios/engine.ts`, run with `SCENARIO_CONFIG`) under the stated conditions — not predictions, not backtest results, and not claims that any real trader realized them.
>
> **Reproducibility:** every P&L figure below was generated by executing the engine on the stated inputs. Given the same inputs, the engine returns these exact numbers on every run — that is the point of deterministic stress testing.

Each walkthrough reconstructs:
1. A realistic natural-language trader thesis.
2. A **stated, assumed** off-hours market state (token price, reference close, basis dislocation, orderbook liquidity).
3. The deterministic stress shocks and adversarial counter-arguments produced by the engine.
4. The desk policy verdict (`WAIT` / `REDUCE` / `REJECT`) that follows deterministically from those inputs.
5. An **illustrative outcome under these conditions** — what would happen to this position *if* the assumed market state resolved the way described.

Shock parameters are documented — with sources and assumptions separated — in [`SHOCK_CALIBRATION_METHODOLOGY.md`](./SHOCK_CALIBRATION_METHODOLOGY.md).

## Scenario Walkthrough 1: Tesla (rTSLA) — Weekend Hype Premium

### Stated Conditions (assumptions, not observations)
- **Session:** Saturday, Weekend Off-Hours (≈47 hours until Monday 09:30 ET cash open).
- **Background premise:** weekend social chatter about robotaxi regulatory news; retail sentiment drives wrapper demand while market makers cannot hedge in the underlying until Monday.
- **Assumed reference state:** NASDAQ closed Friday at **$217.80**.
- **Assumed token state (`rTSLAUSDT`):** weekend hype drives the token to **$226.10** — a **+3.81% basis premium** over the reference close.
- **Assumed liquidity:** Level-1 spread **$0.48 (0.21%)**, top-of-book depth thinned ~60% (sizes ~12/12).

### Trader Input
> *"I want to go long $25,000 on rTSLA this Saturday morning. The robotaxi regulatory leaks look massive, and I want to front-run the institutional crowd before the stock market opens on Monday."*

### Desk Stress Engine Output (engine-executed figures)
1. **Adversarial Red Team Challenge:**
   - *Core Vulnerability:* The trader assumes Saturday sentiment will translate into Monday cash buying pressure.
   - *Counter-Argument:* Retail wrapper buying on weekend exchanges is un-anchored — market makers cannot hedge in underlying stock until Monday. The +3.81% premium is a structural tax on weekend buyers: if Monday cash opens flat, the token suffers the full basis crush regardless of news sentiment.
2. **Deterministic Shock Scenarios** (shock parameters per `SHOCK_CALIBRATION_METHODOLOGY.md`; β to BTC for `rTSLA` = 0.40):
   - **Market Risk (-5%):** shocked reference **$206.91** → shocked token **$214.80** | P&L **-$1,250.00 (-5.00%)**
   - **Crypto Contagion (-8% BTC × β 0.40):** shocked token **$218.86** | P&L **-$800.00 (-3.20%)**
   - **Token Microstructure (300 bps basis widening + 50% depth cut):** shocked token **$219.57** | P&L **-$722.46 (-2.89%)**
   - **Combined Shock:** shocked reference **$206.91** → shocked token **$201.91** | P&L **-$2,674.38 (-10.70%)**
3. **Desk Policy Verdict:** **`WAIT`**
   - *Decisive Reason:* Severe off-hours basis premium (+3.81%) with ~47 hours of unanchored gap exposure; combined-shock drawdown (-10.70%) breaches the desk's gating threshold.

### Illustrative Outcome Under These Conditions
- *If* Monday cash opens roughly flat (say TSLA at **$218.10**, +0.14% vs Friday's close) and arbitrageurs re-align the wrapper within minutes of the open, the token converges from **$226.10 toward ~$218.30**.
- A $25,000 position bought at the assumed Saturday premium would in that case lose **≈ -$862 (-3.45%)** in the first minutes — purely to basis crush, even though the stock itself opened slightly positive.
- This is exactly the loss mode the desk's `WAIT` verdict is designed to avoid: same news view, materially better entry price after the cash market re-anchors. Note the engine's combined-shock figure (-$2,674) quantifies the worse tail: the flat open is the *benign* resolution.

## Scenario Walkthrough 2: NVIDIA (rNVDA) — Pre-Earnings Weekend Premium

### Stated Conditions (assumptions, not observations)
- **Session:** Sunday, Weekend Off-Hours.
- **Background premise:** pre-earnings speculation on Asian desks lifts the wrapper ahead of a Monday-adjacent earnings print.
- **Assumed reference state:** NASDAQ closed Friday at **$129.37**.
- **Assumed token state (`rNVDAUSDT`):** **$134.80** — a **+4.20% basis premium**.
- **Assumed liquidity:** spread **$0.32 (0.24%)**, depth classified `THIN` (sizes ~4/4).

### Trader Input
> *"Buying $30,000 rNVDA now on Sunday. Earnings run-up is guaranteed, and I don't want to chase on Monday morning."*

### Desk Stress Engine Output (engine-executed figures; β to BTC for `rNVDA` = 0.20)
1. **Adversarial Red Team Challenge:**
   - *Core Vulnerability:* The trader conflates fundamental earnings anticipation with off-hours token pricing.
   - *Counter-Argument:* Paying a +4.20% premium means the position needs the token to rally over 4.2% just to break even against the reference. "Guaranteed" run-ups are precisely when weekend premiums peak.
2. **Deterministic Shock Scenarios:**
   - **Market Risk (-5%):** shocked token **$128.06** | P&L **-$1,500.00 (-5.00%)**
   - **Crypto Contagion (-8% BTC × β 0.20):** shocked token **$132.64** | P&L **-$480.00 (-1.60%)**
   - **Token Microstructure (300 bps basis widening + 50% depth cut):** shocked token **$130.92** | P&L **-$863.70 (-2.88%)**
   - **Combined Shock:** shocked token **$122.38** | P&L **-$2,763.39 (-9.21%)**
3. **Desk Policy Verdict:** **`WAIT`**
   - *Decisive Reason:* `BASIS_DECOUPLING` (>3.0% threshold) plus `OFF_HOURS_LIQUIDITY`.

### Illustrative Outcome Under These Conditions
- *If* Monday cash opens near Friday's close and the basis vanishes within minutes of the bell, the premium paid on Sunday (on a $30,000 position, roughly **-$1,113**, ~3.7%, as the token converges toward ~$129.80) is the cost of impatience — before any earnings surprise is even considered.
- The deterministic point of this walkthrough: the desk does not need to predict the earnings outcome to justify `WAIT` — the unanchored premium alone is quantified risk with no compensating edge during the closure.

## Scenario Walkthrough 3: Crypto Contagion Spillover (rCOIN)

### Stated Conditions (assumptions, not observations)
- **Session:** Monday pre-open, 04:00 AM ET — U.S. cash market still closed; token trading continuously.
- **Background premise:** a weekend global crypto liquidation event (in the spirit of historical crypto deleveraging episodes such as the August 2024 yen-carry unwind) bleeds into crypto-venue pricing.
- **Assumed state:** BTC has drawn down sharply over the weekend; token wrappers carry high beta to crypto flows. `rCOIN` β to BTC = **0.75** (exchange revenue tied to crypto volumes).

### Trader Input
> *"Going long $20,000 on rCOIN at 04:00 AM ET Monday before US open. Coinbase stock has nothing to do with Japan rates, so this dip is free money."*

### Desk Stress Engine Output (engine-executed figures)
1. **Adversarial Red Team Challenge:**
   - *Counter-Argument:* The corporate-fundamentals framing ignores venue mechanics — `rCOIN` carries a high beta to crypto stress, and during off-hours liquidations, margin systems liquidate crypto-denominated token positions regardless of the underlying company's earnings.
2. **Deterministic Stress Scenarios** (entry and reference both $250.00, basis flat pre-open):
   - **Market Risk (-5%):** shocked token **$237.50** | P&L **-$1,000.00 (-5.00%)**
   - **Crypto Contagion (-8% BTC × β 0.75):** shocked token **$235.00** | P&L **-$1,200.00 (-6.00%)**
   - **Token Microstructure (300 bps basis widening + 50% depth cut):** shocked token **$242.50** | P&L **-$600.00 (-3.00%)**
   - **Combined Shock:** shocked token **$216.55** | P&L **-$2,675.80 (-13.38%)**
3. **Desk Policy Verdict:** **`REJECT / WAIT`**
   - *Decisive Reason:* High contagion beta during an unanchored global margin event; combined-shock drawdown (-13.38%) is far beyond the gating threshold.

### Illustrative Outcome Under These Conditions
- *If* the underlying opens sharply lower when U.S. markets open (in the August 2024 episode, COIN fell double digits on the open amid the broader selloff), buyers of the 04:00 AM "dip" in token wrappers would have faced immediate further downside with thinned liquidity — the worst combination for exits.
- The desk's contribution is not predicting the open; it is refusing to bless a position whose worst case is dominated by a structural, quantifiable contagion channel.
