BITGET AIREDTEAM DESK

Identity standards

The mark is a token sheared along a fault. How far the two halves have moved is not a styling decision — it is the reading the desk returns. This document exists so that stays true everywhere the brand appears.

01 — THE IDEA

The mark carries the metric

Tokenized US equities keep trading for 65.5 hours a week after NYSE and Nasdaq close. Across that window the token drifts away from the thing it is supposed to track. The mark is that event: an octagonal token, cut by a fault plane at −12°, with the two halves displaced along it.

Because displacement is a parameter rather than a drawing, the same construction produces the whole verdict set. The logo is the reference cut at 9u. A reader who learns the scale inside the product then reads the logo differently outside it.

06 — THE FAULT DEVICE

A measured rule at −12°

The fault leaves the mark and becomes a layout device: a short red rule at the same angle, used to close a text block. It has a defined length. It is never a full-bleed sweep across a layout, because at −12° a long line drops far enough vertically to cross whatever else is on the page.

It never crosses type. A red line through a headline reads as a strike-through — the layout appears to cancel its own message.

The overhang rule is the second device: 168 hours with 65.5 of them shaded. It is the product's central fact drawn as data, and it carries a deck, a billboard or a slide on its own.

07 — MISUSE

What breaks the computation

  1. Do not change the displacement outside the verdict scale. Offset is data. An arbitrary offset states a risk reading that the engine never returned.
  2. Do not recolour the seam. Except to a verdict colour, in a verdict glyph. The seam is the only red surface in the system.
  3. Do not rotate, stretch, outline or shade the mark. −12° is fixed. The mark has no depth; it is a plan view of a fracture.
  4. Do not let anything enter the clearspace. 18 units on all sides, the same value as the chamfer.
  5. Do not set figures in the sans. The mono face is the claim that a number was computed rather than narrated. It is the product's core promise.
